AEROPULSE PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy for AeroPulse

Last updated: October 15, 2026

AeroPulse: Breath, Allergy & Runner Radar ("AeroPulse", "we", "our", or "us") provides atmospheric and space weather forecasts to help runners, parents, and individuals with respiratory conditions identify optimal outdoor safe windows. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed by the application.

1. Information We Access

  • Location Data: When enabled, AeroPulse accesses your device's geographical coordinates (GPS) solely to query hyper-local air quality (PM2.5, PM10, ozone, pollen counts), meteorological conditions, and sunrise/sunset timings. Your location is processed on-device and forwarded directly to open meteorological API endpoints. We do not store, track, log, or sell your location history.
  • Saved Locations: Custom favorite cities and trailheads you search and save are stored entirely locally on your device within an encrypted Android Room Database cache.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

AeroPulse utilizes trusted external open APIs to provide scientific telemetry:

  • Open-Meteo Air Quality & Weather API: Fetches European and US air quality indices, particulate matter, botanical pollen levels (grass, ragweed, birch, alder), and solar UV forecasts.
  • NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC): Fetches planetary geomagnetic Kp-index and solar storm condition feeds.
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding: Resolves city names when searching for favorite locations.

3. Data Retention and Security

All user preferences, selected profiles, and cached favorite locations remain on your device and are cleared whenever you clear app data or uninstall the application. No personal user accounts or identity credentials are required.

4. Children's Privacy

AeroPulse does not collect personally identifiable information from any user, including children under 13 years of age.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the application and at this URL.
